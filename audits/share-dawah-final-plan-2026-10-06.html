The existing ledger can support the complete product

The original audit correctly identifies trust, privacy, attribution and eligibility defects. Its execution plan needs stronger transaction boundaries, consistent browser measurement, persisted downstream attribution, full correction handling and a broader rewards contract to meet your clarified scope.

Plain language

The existing system is useful: it records links, visits and community actions. We should repair and extend it, then give administrators a complete way to offer thank-you recognition, points, badges, money and benefits. A reward must have a clear rule, real evidence, a visible status and a way to correct mistakes.

Retain aiarmada/affiliates for attributed facts, fraud, cash accounting and payouts; signals for a defined analytics reader; links for real branded redirects; and existing communications infrastructure for delivery. Add app-owned program policy, award/point/benefit ledgers and workflow adapters. Package extensions stay generic; Dawah vocabulary stays in presentation.

One journey, with an accountable reward trail

1 · ShareGuest or member gets a branded link and an accurate majlis card.
2 · RespondA recipient opens the link and registers or takes another real action.
3 · UnderstandThe sharer sees defined, private impact and optional calm feedback.
4 · ReceiveConfigured program rules determine recognition, points, money or benefits.
Plain language

A guest can sign in and claim earlier sharing. A member can see why a reward is pending, approved, paid, redeemed or corrected. People remain free to share without joining a reward program. Administrators can explain every award and resolve a dispute without guessing.

Technical description

Each server-confirmed business transition produces one source identity, attribution decision and optional conversion. Committed valid facts feed program evaluation. Every award references its source facts and frozen policy version; each effect has its own stable key. Signup → first-share is measured as a cohort transition, not a fabricated delivery event.

Suggested Malay copy: “Aktiviti direkodkan melalui pautan anda.” Show visits, registrations and recorded check-ins separately. For stronger attendance wording, require the configured attendance-verification standard.

Do not say “you brought someone,” “people benefited” or “learning achieved” from an attribution record alone. Those are causal or educational claims the system cannot prove.

Reuse ledgers; separate facts from entitlements

Plain language

A visit is a fact. A reward is a promise made under a specific program. Paying that promise is a third step. Keep these separate so changing a reward rule cannot rewrite what happened, and correcting an event cannot quietly erase a payment.

Technical description

Three domains: affiliate attribution/conversions as fact authority; app program policy + append-only reward entries as entitlement authority; affiliates monetary accounting/payouts as cash authority. Points and benefits have their own units and fulfillment records. Signals and cached counters are projections, never new attribution authorities.

Proposed app records and invariants
  • Source attribution receipt: source type/transition key, immutable attribution reference or explicit unattributed decision, source occurred-at, provenance, definition version. Avoid a duplicate receipt where the source record already holds and enforces this invariant.
  • Guest association: guest affiliate/profile UUID, claimant UUID, private capability verifier, expiry/revocation and claim timestamps. Unique profile claim; historical affiliate owner does not change.
  • Reward program/version: program family, status, effective dates, immutable rules, component list, stacking rules, eligibility, caps, budget, hold period, terms and review requirements.
  • Award: recipient, source identity, program family, component, policy version, value/unit, evidence snapshot and current status. Unique recipient + source identity + program family + component; policy versions do not create new awards.
  • Award entries: immutable evaluation, hold, approval, rejection, reversal and correction records with actor/reason/time/source event. Multi-event milestone awards additionally reference every contributing fact and a deterministic qualification-cycle key.
  • Points/badge/benefit records: append-only point credits/debits/expiry, private badge grants/revocations, benefit inventory/reservations and redemption fulfillment. Link all entries to an award or redemption operation.
  • Effect receipts: durable pending/leased/succeeded/failed/cancelled work keyed by source event + effect type + recipient/program. Store minimum payload; dispatcher can recover committed unsent work.

New schemas use UUID primary/foreign IDs, unique/index constraints for race safety, no foreign-key constraints/cascades and no SoftDeletes. Operational lifecycles use status plus dedicated immutable transition timestamps, not flags or hidden JSON states. Keep proposed models in existing application directories. Follow the project’s clean-cutover migration convention; this review does not reset or upgrade any live database.

Split only the contracts that callers actually need: link/outbound, attribution/outcome, lifecycle and analytics reads. Keep null implementations. Large façade and enum cleanup can be completed in this delivery but is not a prerequisite for fixing raw IPs or disabled-link credit. Removing API fields requires web/mobile schema parity and coordinated consumers.

One committed fact, recoverable effects

Plain language

If two requests arrive together, only one records the action. If a server stops halfway, it leaves either a complete record or no record. If it stops after saving but before sending a notification, a worker finds the unfinished delivery and safely continues.

Technical description

One connection/transaction contains source decision, conversion creation, fraud/accounting, award/budget reservation where evaluated synchronously, projections and effect receipts. Cross-connection work uses committed keyed receipts and reconciliation. External sends happen outside the transaction after commit.

  1. Freeze the source decision. Source workflows expose created/no-op and immutable transition identity. Persist attribution or “unattributed” when the transition occurs; repeats cannot acquire credit from a newly opened link.
  2. Resolve and recheck eligibility. Lock authoritative profile/link and source rows in a documented order. Staff check-in uses the admission’s provenance. Unknown/disallowed source evidence fails closed for rewards.
  3. Use one canonical unique key. Package lookup, insertion and collision recovery use the same namespaced caller-supplied identity. Preserve separate commercial namespaces and owner boundaries.
  4. Make collision recovery PostgreSQL-safe. Use atomic insert-on-conflict or an explicitly rolled-back savepoint before querying. Retrying deadlocks does not repair an aborted transaction after a unique violation.
  5. Commit all database effects together. Return created/replayed/rejected or no-attribution explicitly. Only creation changes projections; rejection earns no valid credit. Package event replay must not duplicate app effects.
  6. Deliver through durable work. After-commit dispatch plus a periodic dispatcher for pending work closes the crash gap. Consumers recheck source validity, program version, preferences and suppression before fulfillment. Retries are allowed; historical bulk notification/award replay is not.
  7. Correct through explicit transitions. Cancellation, submission rejection, attendance correction, confirmed fraud and eligible unsave/unfollow events update validity and append correction evidence. Award reversal wins races with queued evaluation/notification; projection refresh is idempotent.

For first-visit races without an established browser credential, the system cannot safely prove two simultaneous requests are one browser. Do not substitute IP fingerprints. Establish a random first-party capability, label browser counts as estimates, and test the subsequent established-capability race.

Exactly-once limit: database facts and entitlements can be uniquely recorded. External email, payout or benefit delivery may be retried after ambiguous provider timeouts; stable provider operation keys and reconciliation prevent a blind second send/payment. Where a provider has no idempotency/status support, hold ambiguity for operator review.

Plain language

Administrators choose what qualifies and what is offered. They cannot spend more than a funded program budget, award the same event repeatedly, or quietly change yesterday’s rules. Members can see the rules before joining and can appeal a decision.

Technical description

Programs have immutable effective versions and selectable components. Each program family caps awards per canonical source and recipient. Version is evidence, not a uniqueness dimension. Cross-program stacking requires an explicit rule; default is one reward family for the same source/component. Combine rate/cap/budget checks under locks with award creation.

Qualification rules for the complete engine
  • Connection: signup, save/follow/going and saved-search actions can inform impact and recognition. No cash/benefits for visits, outbound intent or unverified signups by default.
  • Participation: accepted registration and verified attendance can qualify. Distinguish participant from purchaser; scope identity to registration/admission/occurrence. Staff-assisted evidence is not a referral by the staff member.
  • Contribution: accepted event/content submissions qualify after moderation; submitted-only activity remains a historical action. Source rejection removes qualification.
  • Advanced rules: supported configuration for subjects/categories, date windows, minimum verification, first/recurring outcomes, participant/sharer caps, hold time, fixed cash or point amount, component stacking, benefit choice and manual approval. Validation rejects conflicting/circular rules.
  • Milestones: deterministic counts of qualifying source facts; no streak or urgency mechanics. Retain contributing source references, and re-evaluate/revoke the milestone when those sources are corrected.
  • Suspension: pause new qualification/fulfillment on confirmed fraud or program pause; existing valid obligations stay visible. Dismissing a fraud signal alone does not approve an award. Human overrides require role, reason and evidence.
  • Guest claim: attribution facts can exist before login. No reward component is issued to an unclaimed guest. After claim, enrollment and applicable identity/payee checks, eligible pre-claim evidence may qualify under the published claim/enrollment window. Re-evaluation is limited to provably owned facts within the program’s published eligibility window, never a bulk historical replay.

Award lifecycle: evaluated → held/pending review → approved or rejected → fulfilled, with explicit reversed/corrected paths. Recognition fulfillment is acknowledgement; points fulfillment is a ledger credit; cash fulfillment is a confirmed payment; benefits fulfillment is confirmed delivery. Component status does not imply another component was delivered.

Program lifecycle: draft → ready → active → paused → closed. Activation requires validated rules, terms and eligibility; monetary/benefit modes additionally require funded budget, supported fulfillment, retention and reversal policy. Editing an active rule creates a new version; old awards retain their original rule/version.

Cash posting and payout contract

Use existing CreatePayout, payout reservation/operation records, processor contract and reversal action. Place canonical payout orchestration in the domain package rather than making app jobs depend on a Filament UI action. Resolve every reward recipient to the authenticated claimant/member. Cash posts to that recipient’s explicitly marked share/reward affiliate, separately from any commercial affiliate. Guest fact-profile ownership stays unchanged; a frozen association maps the source evidence to the recipient. Keep cash balances, budgets, caps and payouts separate per currency (MYR is the planning default); do not sum currencies or silently convert. Reward payouts explicitly select reward-award IDs; generic commercial payout/bonus paths must not process this profile. Automated providers require credential/readiness checks and tested reconciliation before activation.

Budget invariant: allocated funded value = available value + reserved unpaid obligations + net confirmed spent value. Net spent is gross confirmed spend minus actual confirmed recoveries; each obligation occupies one category. Include approved/held unpaid obligations in reservations, and include fulfilled cash/benefit costs in spent value. Concurrency locks program/budget and award rows in a fixed order. Release only genuinely cancelled unpaid reservations. Do not count uncertain provider operations as available money.

After payment or redemption: reversal does not pretend money or a consumed benefit returned. Preserve original fulfillment; create a recoverable adjustment/receivable case, freeze disputed future fulfillment if policy allows, and require an authorized settlement decision. Never debit a bank account automatically. Never credit an already-paid reversal back into the program budget before actual recovery. Existing reversal rejects an open payout reservation; cancel/reconcile that reservation before correction. [E8]

Points: integer points with explicit earning/expiry rules; point-only programs need no payout account. Redeemable points are liabilities within the benefit budget. No point-to-cash conversion unless a separately configured, funded exchange policy explicitly enables it, with immutable rate/value snapshot and caps. Transfer/sale of points is off by default. Spending reversals create a dispute/adjustment rather than silently inventing a negative cash balance.

Commercial safety: cash mode activation must prevent generic monthly bonus/downline jobs or unfiltered payout screens from processing share profiles unintentionally. Mark profile purpose, scope all program/accounting queries, and test commercial record preservation in deletion/restoration. Program pause, account deletion and payee change must preserve already-established monetary obligations. Terminal closure follows the defined retention/settlement policy.

Plain language

  • Growth reader: landing cohorts → signup → first committed share → accepted participation, by channel/origin/campaign/content type and guest/auth state. Include denominator, maturity, unknowns, quality category, freshness and fraud exclusions. Keep reward value, actual cash paid and attribution counts separate.
  • Scale: database aggregate/pagination queries and bounded detail pages, not loading a user’s lifetime collections and calculating every link in memory. Suitable composite indexes and idempotent projections; hand reconciliation and representative query plans prove correctness/performance.

Measure repeat sharing and eligible signup-to-first-share rates, with sample sizes and predeclared windows. Establish baseline before setting uplift targets. Full feature delivery does not prove a behavioral benefit; a controlled comparison can support causal claims when volume permits. Complaints, opt-outs, invalid rewards, payout ambiguity and latency are guardrails.
